# ADR-118 — One catalog of the designs, and an import that merges catalogs

Status: Implemented in the 2026-10-28 working trees of the app, Slidr Library and Slidr Studio; **not committed** at the time of this record, the library **not deployed**, and the published library **not folded** yet.

## Request

Shown the Ready slides of Slidr Library filtered by "All · 344 / Written by hand · 286 / From the studio · 58": "The split has no meaning any more. Do away with the separate files, update the documentation, and the library needs no such filter: it is a historical split without meaning." And: "Support merging the catalogs instead of replacing them" when a library brings in an archive of another (Slidr Library's README, "Exporting and importing").

## Context

- The designs were two catalogs. `elements/designs/index.json` (42 MB, 286 designs) was written by the app's generators in an author's copy ([ADR-079](ADR-079-elements-designs.md)); `managed.json` (12 MB, 58 designs) by Slidr Studio's publications, so that the studio never wrote over the other. Since [ADR-114](ADR-114-no-copy-of-the-library.md) there is no author's copy and no generator writes `index.json`: every new design comes from the studio, through the library's API.
- The two differed in more than their place. The studio's held the names of its designs and groups, the pixel sizes of their pictures and their search tags; the first designs had theirs in the app's messages, `images/designs/sizes.json` and `tags/designs.json`. The app merged the two as it read them, and the library's page showed which design came from which.
- A translation was taken only of the studio's designs: the first ones were "translated with the app's scripts", which no longer publish.
- Bringing in an archive wrote each catalog over the library's (Slidr Library's README, "Exporting and importing"). A library that published designs, tags or samples of its own lost them from its catalogs, though their pictures stayed.
- A Worker holds 128 MB. One catalog of every design is some 54 MB; a publication that read it and wrote it again whole would hold it twice.

## Decision

- **One catalog**, `elements/designs/index.json`: `groups`, `groupIds`, `slides`, `subjects`, and `names` and `groupNames` in Hebrew and English for the designs and groups the app's messages do not name. Every picture's size is in `images/designs/sizes.json`, and every design's tags in `tags/designs.json`. The per-language files are `elements/designs/<language>/index.json` only; `revs.json` names the one catalog (and lists its designs in `catalogs`, as the studio reads it).
- **Slidr Library folds a library of before** (`foldManaged`, `server/core/media/designCatalog.ts`) at the start of every publication: of uploads, of an author's files, or of an archive. `managed.json`'s designs go after the others as the app merged them (a group the catalog has takes them after its designs; another comes after its groups; the catalog's design wins on an id), their sizes into `sizes.json`, their tags beside those of `tags/designs.json`; then `managed.json` and its files of each language are taken away. **Try again** does it with no upload.
- **Publishing writes into the one catalog by its structure.** `designs: add` puts a design in its group (a new group named as the upload names it), and its picture's size and tags beside the catalog; `designs: translate` takes any design. Only the members but `slides` are parsed; a design's slides are parsed where they change. The catalog is written as parts of the bytes it was read from with what changed between them, hashed over the parts (SHA-256 in JavaScript, checked against WebCrypto), and given to R2 in a Worker as a stream of a known length (`FixedLengthStream`), a megabyte at a time; the history keeps the file it replaces by streaming it from one bucket to the other. In Node the parts are joined.
- **An archive merges into the catalogs a library's publications write** (`server/core/media/merged.ts`, `archiveMerge.ts`), and writes over every other file: the catalog of the designs a design at a time, `sizes.json` and `images/designs/thumbs/index.json` an entry at a time, `tags/<part>.json` an item and a language at a time, and the samples of `templates/catalog.json` a template and a language at a time. Where both have one, the archive's wins; what only the library has stays. An archive of a library of before is folded before it is merged. The page's preview counts the files it merges apart from those it replaces.
- **The app reads one catalog** (`designs.ts`), and `managed.json` after it while a library still has one (`mergeCatalogs` takes any number of catalogs, in order). The names of a design come from the app's messages, else the catalog, else its id. `Designs.managed` is gone.
- **The library's page shows the designs as one**: no filter of their source, no "written by hand"/"published from the studio", no catalog file in a design's details. The upload form offers every design to translate, and every group to a new one.

## Consequences

- The published library is folded by its first publication after the library is deployed: **Publish → Try again** in Slidr Library. In a Worker, the fold holds the two catalogs' bytes (54 MB) and writes the one catalog from them as a stream; a publication of a design holds the catalog's bytes once.
- Every publication of a design now changes the one catalog, so the words of every design are made again (`revs.json`), not those of the studio's alone: 344 designs in 16 languages. In Node, the fold of the published library with all its words took 2 s.
- Releases of the app before this one read the names of the studio's designs only from `managed.json`: in a folded library they show those 58 by their ids. Their pictures are drawn at their sizes, which `sizes.json` now holds, and their tags are found in `tags/designs.json`.
- The ids of designs added through the library still start with `m-`: it keeps them apart from the first designs' without reading the catalog. `designs.test.ts` tells the two apart by it ("the 296 first designs").
- Slidr Studio reads the one catalog for planning, and `managed.json` beside it while a library still has one; the catalog of a drawing is `index.json` unless `revs.json` or such a library says otherwise.

## Verification

- Slidr Library: `pnpm test`, 203 pass: the catalog read by its structure and written as read where nothing changed; a design put in, moved and translated; another catalog's designs appended in the app's order; SHA-256 over parts at every block boundary, and the stream a Worker writes; a library of before folded by **Try again** (sizes, tags, groups, names, per-language files and words), and a translation of a design of `managed.json` in the same publication; the undo at every write of a publication of a design; an archive merged into a library with designs, tags, sizes, words of pictures and a sample of its own, and nothing written when it is brought in again; an archive of before into a library of before.
- On the scratch copy of the published library (42 MB and 12 MB): the fold through `slidr-library import`, with the words and the files of each language, took 2 s, and the folded library read by the app's `mergeCatalogs` is the two of before, design for design: 344 designs, 21 groups, their order, slides, subjects, names, group names, sizes and tags; `revs.json` and the 16 files of each language of the one catalog. An archive exported on 2026-10-27, of before, brought into an empty library gives the same.
- In Edge against that library: the Ready slides without the filter, the studio's groups and designs named by the catalog, a design of the studio up close with its tags and its picture's crop; the upload form offering all 344 designs to translate.
- The app: `designCatalog.test.ts` (8), `designThumbs.test.ts`, `build/media.test.ts` and `tags.test.ts` pass; `designs.test.ts` fails the same two tests on the published library's content as before ("296 first designs" finds 286, and the group of invitations has one design). Slidr Studio: `cargo test`, 58 pass, and its pages' type check.
